StrideIQ Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 30, 2026 · Last updated: November 12, 2026
App: StrideIQ for Android and Wear OS (package com.strideiq.app) · Developer: Tervaro

In short: StrideIQ stores your health, fitness, nutrition and progress data on your own device. There are no StrideIQ accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Data leaves your phone only for the optional online features described in sections 4 and 5. We do not sell your data.

1. Information StrideIQ accesses and collects

Depending on the features you use and the permissions you grant, StrideIQ handles:

  • Activity and fitness data: step counts, distance, calories burned (total and active), exercise sessions and their routes, read from Health Connect (section 2).
  • Health data: heart rate and body weight read from Health Connect; heart rate measured by the StrideIQ watch app during workouts you start.
  • Phone step counter (optional "Native Beta"): readings from your phone's built-in step sensor, using the Physical activity permission.
  • Watch data (optional): steps and workouts recorded by the StrideIQ Wear OS app, sent from your watch to your phone directly over the Wear OS connection between your own devices, not through our servers.
  • Run location (optional, Wear OS watch): when you start a run in the StrideIQ watch app and allow location access, the watch records your GPS position (latitude, longitude, accuracy, altitude, speed, direction and time) while the run is in progress, until you finish it. StrideIQ uses it to calculate distance, pace and your route. Before asking for location access the watch app explains this on a "Run GPS" screen; if you choose "Not now" or deny access, the run is recorded without location.
  • Samsung Health export files (optional): if you choose to import a Samsung Health export, StrideIQ reads it on your phone and keeps daily steps, distance, calories, active time, minute step counts, exercise sessions and GPS routes, heart rate, weight and sleep start/end times. Other data in the export is skipped.
  • Sleep (optional): sleep nights you log by hand (bedtime and wake time). If you turn on automatic sleep estimates and allow Android's Usage access for StrideIQ, the phone app also reads the times your screen turned on and off, the times your phone was unlocked, and the times it was switched off and on, for about the last 9 days, only while StrideIQ is open (see "Sleep tracking and Usage access" in section 3).
  • Nutrition data you enter: meals, foods, recipes, targets, barcode lookups, food search text, food descriptions you type for AI food analysis, and food photos.
  • Progress photos and body measurements you take or enter in the app.
  • Profile details you choose to enter, such as date of birth and sex, plus goals and settings.

StrideIQ has no user accounts, no sign-in and no cloud sync. StrideIQ uses your location only (a) during runs you start in the StrideIQ watch app, and (b) as part of exercise routes already recorded in Health Connect or a Samsung Health export. It does not request background location access and does not use location outside a run you started.

2. Health Connect data and permissions

StrideIQ only reads from Health Connect. It never writes, changes or deletes Health Connect data. It asks for these permissions:

PermissionWhat StrideIQ uses it for
StepsDaily step totals, goals, streaks, charts and the home-screen widget
DistanceDaily distance and workout distance
Total calories burnedDaily energy totals and nutrition balance
Active calories burnedActive energy per day and per workout
ExerciseYour workout history
Exercise routesShowing a workout's route on a map (Health Connect asks you for each route)
Heart rateWorkout heart-rate charts and zones
WeightYour weight trend and nutrition targets
Read health data in the backgroundUpdating your daily totals, the widget and goal notifications about every 15 minutes while the app is closed

StrideIQ does not request sleep data from Health Connect.

Data read from Health Connect is copied into StrideIQ's private storage on your device so it can be shown and analysed. The only Health Connect data that can leave your device is your most recent weight. It is included in a workout calorie estimate if you use that optional online feature (section 4).

The use of information received from Health Connect will adhere to the Health Connect Permissions policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not use Health Connect data for advertising, do not sell it, and do not use it to determine creditworthiness or for lending.

3. How we use fitness, health and nutrition information

Only to provide the features you see in the app:

  • Daily summaries, goals, streaks, records, charts, the widget and goal notifications
  • Workout history, heart-rate zones and calorie estimates
  • Nutrition logging and targets
  • Progress photo comparisons and body measurement trends
  • Sleep history and averages, and the morning "How did you sleep?" estimate

It is not used for advertising, profiling or marketing, and it is not used to train AI models by us.

Sleep tracking and Usage access

Sleep tracking is optional. You can log, edit and delete sleep by hand in Evolve → Sleep without giving StrideIQ any permission.

Automatic sleep estimates are off until you turn them on. Before Android's Usage access screen opens, StrideIQ shows a screen explaining what is read and why. If you allow Usage access:

  • What is read: only the times your screen turned on and off, the times your phone was unlocked, and the times your phone was switched off and on. On Android versions that allow it, StrideIQ asks Android for these event types only. On other versions Android hands over a wider list of usage events, and StrideIQ's phone code immediately throws away everything except these times, before anything reaches the rest of the app.
  • What is not read or kept: which apps you use, app names, how long you use them, messages, notifications or anything on your screen. These are never stored, logged or sent anywhere.
  • When: only while StrideIQ is open (when you open it or switch back to it). StrideIQ does not run a background service to watch your screen.
  • Why: to estimate when you fell asleep and woke up. StrideIQ looks for the longest stretch without phone use around your usual sleep time (default 11:00 PM–7:00 AM, adjustable in Settings) and shows it as an estimate for you to confirm, edit or dismiss.
  • Where: estimates are made on your phone only. Screen and unlock times are used in memory and are not saved. StrideIQ saves only the resulting sleep night: bedtime and wake time (the estimate and, if you changed it, your corrected times), whether you confirmed it, and a few numbers about the estimate (such as confidence). Sleep data is never sent to StrideIQ's server or to anyone else.
  • Turning it off: switch off Settings → Sleep tracking → Automatic sleep estimates (StrideIQ then stops reading), or turn off Usage access for StrideIQ in Android settings. Manual sleep logging keeps working either way.
  • Deleting: delete or remove a night in Evolve → Sleep, use Settings → Delete range / Delete everything, or clear the app's storage / uninstall the app. Sleep imported from a Samsung Health export is shown in Evolve → Sleep but kept exactly as imported.

Sleep estimates are approximate. They measure when you stopped and started using your phone, not sleep itself, and are not a medical measurement.

4. AI food analysis and other AI-assisted features

StrideIQ's online features require an access token issued by the developer, entered in the app. Without one, no data is sent to the StrideIQ service or to OpenAI.

  • AI food analysis (only when you tap Analyze food): a description of what you ate that you type (up to 1,000 characters), a meal photo (JPEG), or both are sent to the StrideIQ service, which forwards them to OpenAI to estimate the foods and their nutrition. The StrideIQ service does not store your description or photo; it temporarily keeps the AI-generated result (section 8). The photo is optional: you can analyze a description alone, a photo alone, or both together as one meal. Only what you entered for that meal is sent. When you scan a barcode and choose to read the nutrition label, only the label photo is sent. The estimate is returned to your phone. Nothing is added to your food log until you tap Save meal. The meal is then saved on your phone together with the description you typed and the AI's notes.
  • Workout calorie estimate: when the online service is configured (an access token has been entered), an estimate request is sent automatically when a workout recorded with StrideIQ finishes. There is currently no separate switch for this. It includes:
    • the workout type and label
    • start and end times, time-zone offset, durations and pauses
    • heart-rate samples and summary values recorded during the workout
    • your most recent weight and the date it was measured, if available (this weight may come from Health Connect)
    • your age in whole years (your date of birth is not sent) and your sex, if you have set them
    Your name, location (including GPS locations recorded during runs), routes, photos, notes and device identifiers are not sent. OpenAI returns an estimate, which is stored with the workout on your phone.
  • Food search and barcodes: the text you search for and scanned barcode numbers are sent to the StrideIQ service, which looks them up in the U.S. Department of Agriculture's FoodData Central database. Barcode numbers are also sent directly from your phone to Open Food Facts.

Progress photos, body measurements, Samsung Health imports and Health Connect data other than weight are not sent to any AI service. AI estimates are approximate and are not medical advice.

5. Third-party service providers

  • Cloudflare hosts the StrideIQ service (a Cloudflare Worker) that receives the requests described in section 4. Cloudflare's request logging is enabled for this service. Cloudflare processes the requests and keeps these logs under its own terms and retention, which the app does not change: cloudflare.com/privacypolicy.
  • OpenAI analyses the food descriptions, food photos and workout data sent through the StrideIQ service. StrideIQ asks OpenAI not to store the requests and responses (the store: false setting), but we cannot control OpenAI's handling beyond that; OpenAI's own API terms and policies govern what it retains, for example for abuse monitoring: openai.com/policies.
  • USDA FoodData Central receives food search text and barcode numbers from the StrideIQ service.
  • Open Food Facts receives barcode numbers directly from your phone: world.openfoodfacts.org/privacy.
  • OpenStreetMap map tiles are loaded when you view a route map (including, in a later version, runs recorded by the StrideIQ watch app). The tile server receives your IP address and the map area you view, but not your route data: osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy.
  • Google provides Health Connect, the Wear OS connection, the barcode scanner (Google Play services) and, if enabled on your phone, Android backup.

6. On-device storage vs. external processing

Stays on your device:

  • your step, workout, heart-rate, weight and sleep history (including manually logged and estimated sleep)
  • Native Beta step readings and watch data
  • run locations recorded by the watch (stored on the watch and, after they sync over the Wear OS connection between your own devices, on your phone)
  • Samsung Health imports
  • nutrition logs (including the food descriptions you typed for AI food analysis) and food photos
  • progress photos and body measurements
  • goals and settings

On the watch, data stays in the watch app until it has synced to your phone. Run locations are then deleted from the watch automatically once they are more than 30 days old; locations that have not yet reached your phone are never deleted by this clean-up. Other watch data (workout events, heart rate, steps) stays on the watch until you uninstall the watch app or clear its storage.

Processed outside your device: only the items listed in section 4, and map tiles (section 5).

Android backup: the phone app has Android's automatic backup enabled. Depending on your device's backup settings, Android may include StrideIQ's local app data in your device backup and restore it when you reinstall. This can include your workouts, run locations and sleep history. This backup is managed by Google under your account settings, and StrideIQ cannot see or delete it. The watch app is excluded from backup.

Exports: if you export a backup (JSON) or save an image, the file is written where you choose (e.g. Downloads) and is then under your control. A workout export (JSON) includes run locations.

7. Sharing and disclosure

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not share it for advertising.
  • We do not use advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services.

Data is passed only to the service providers in section 5, only to run the feature you use. It may also be disclosed if required by law. When you use the share or export features, data goes only to the people and apps you choose.

8. Retention and deletion

  • On your device: data stays until you delete it or uninstall the app. In the app you can:
    • delete a date range, or use "Delete everything" in Settings. This clears daily, workout (from Health Connect and imports), heart-rate, weight and sleep data, goals, records and import history. It does not clear nutrition, Native Beta, progress photo, body measurement or recorded-workout data.
    • delete all Nutrition data (Nutrition settings)
    • delete all Native Beta data
    • delete individual progress photos and body measurements
    Workouts recorded with the StrideIQ watch or phone workout feature currently cannot be deleted one by one in the app. This includes their run locations. "Delete everything" and "Delete Native Beta data" do not remove them.
  • On the watch: run locations are deleted automatically 30 days after they were recorded, but only once your phone has confirmed it stored them. Other watch data stays until you uninstall the watch app or clear its storage.
  • Clearing the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstalling, removes all StrideIQ data from the phone, including these workouts.
  • The StrideIQ service does not store the original photos, food descriptions, workout data or heart-rate data you send. It does temporarily keep a short record of each request, used to prevent duplicate processing and charges and to apply usage limits:
    • a request ID
    • a one-way hash (SHA-256) of the photo and/or food description, or of the workout data
    • status and timestamps
    • the AI-generated result returned to your phone, or an error message. For food, this result lists the foods, amounts and notes the AI identified, so it can contain information derived from the description you typed or the photo you sent.
    These records are kept only temporarily. They are removed by an automatic clean-up that runs from time to time while the service is in use, typically a few days to about a week after the request. Because the clean-up does not run on a fixed schedule, we cannot guarantee deletion by a specific time, and records can remain longer if the service is not used for a while. Daily usage counts (a date and a number, with no food or workout content) are kept and are not removed by this clean-up. When a request fails, the service writes an error log entry containing a request ID, the access token's label and the error message. It never contains the photo, the food description or workout data. Cloudflare keeps request logs according to its own retention.
  • OpenAI and other providers keep data according to their own policies (section 5); we cannot delete data held by them.
  • Not covered by in-app deletion:
    • data in Health Connect, which belongs to Health Connect and the apps that wrote it (manage it in Health Connect)
    • Android backups (manage them in your Google account)
    • files you exported
    • data held by the providers above
  • Requesting deletion of server records: email cooltom@gmail.com. Requests are handled on a best-effort basis.

9. Security and data transmission

  • All requests to the StrideIQ service, OpenAI, USDA, Open Food Facts and OpenStreetMap use HTTPS (encrypted in transit).
  • The OpenAI key is held only by the StrideIQ service, never in the app.
  • The app's service access token is stored encrypted with a key kept in the Android Keystore.
  • Requests to the service require that token and are rate-limited.
  • Data stored by the app on your device is protected by Android's app sandbox and your device's own encryption and screen lock; StrideIQ does not add its own extra encryption to this data.

No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure.

10. Your choices and permissions

  • Grant or revoke Health Connect permissions at any time in Health Connect settings, including background access.
  • Camera, physical activity, notifications and "Alarms & reminders" are optional and can be turned off in Android settings. The barcode scanner uses Google's scanner and does not need StrideIQ's camera permission.
  • Location on the watch is optional. If you choose "Not now" or deny it, runs still record time and heart rate without GPS. You can change it at any time in the StrideIQ watch app's permission settings on your watch.
  • Usage access (for automatic sleep estimates) is optional. Turn it off in Settings → Sleep tracking or in Android settings; manual sleep logging keeps working.
  • Don't use the online features (section 4) if you don't want data processed outside your device.
  • Export, delete or uninstall as described in section 8.

11. Children's privacy

StrideIQ is not directed to anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly process data from anyone under 18.
